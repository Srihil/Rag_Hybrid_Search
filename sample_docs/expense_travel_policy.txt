1. POLICY OVERVIEW

This policy governs how Meridian employees incur and claim business expenses. All business expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and incurred for legitimate company purposes. Employees must obtain prior approval before committing the company to significant expenditure.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2. EXPENSE CATEGORIES & LIMITS

2.1 Meals & Entertainment

BUSINESS MEALS (client-facing):
- Breakfast: Up to INR 800 per person
- Lunch: Up to INR 1,500 per person
- Dinner: Up to INR 2,500 per person

INTERNAL TEAM MEALS (team events, working lunches):
- Lunch: Up to INR 600 per person
- Dinner: Up to INR 1,000 per person

Alcohol: Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment, up to INR 1,000 per person. Alcohol for internal team meals is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

2.2 Accommodation

DOMESTIC TRAVEL (within India):
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune): Up to INR 7,500 per night
- Other cities: Up to INR 4,500 per night

INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL:
- USA, UK, Europe, Australia: Up to USD/EUR/GBP 200 per night
- Southeast Asia, Middle East: Up to USD 120 per night
- Other regions: Refer to Finance for pre-approval

2.3 Ground Transportation

Taxi/Cab: Use of Uber or Ola is preferred. Company account codes are available from Finance for business travel bookings.
Private car hire: Allowed for airport transfers and inter-city travel where rail is not practical.
Personal car: Mileage reimbursement at INR 12 per kilometre for use of personal vehicle on company business.
Auto-rickshaw, Metro, Bus: Fully reimbursable with receipt.

2.4 Air Travel

All air travel must be booked through the company's travel management portal (travel.meridiantech.com) at least 5 business days in advance for domestic and 10 business days for international.

DOMESTIC (within India): Economy class only.
INTERNATIONAL: Flights under 6 hours — economy class. Flights 6 hours or longer — business class permitted with VP approval.

Frequent flyer points earned on company-paid travel belong to the employee and may be used for personal upgrades. However, upgrades obtained using personal points must not result in the company being billed for the upgrade.

2.5 Communication

International SIM / roaming: Up to INR 3,000 per trip for domestic SIM purchase at destination, or use the company international roaming plan (available via IT).

2.6 Visa & Travel Insurance

Visa fees and mandatory travel insurance are fully reimbursable with supporting receipts. Elective travel insurance beyond mandatory requirements is the employee's personal expense.

3. APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS

All expense claims require manager approval.

ADDITIONAL APPROVAL REQUIRED:
- Single expense > INR 10,000: Department head approval
- Travel outside India: VP approval + HR notification
- Team entertainment > INR 25,000 total: Finance pre-approval
- Expenses outside the categories above: Finance approval before incurring

Pre-approval is NOT the same as expense reimbursement approval. The expense must still be legitimate, within policy, and submitted with receipts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4. EXPENSE SUBMISSION

4.1 Submission Deadline

All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense. Claims submitted after 30 days will require department head approval and may be declined.

4.2 Required Documentation

For all claims: Original receipt or digital invoice showing date, amount, vendor, and nature of expense.
For meals: List of attendees and business purpose must be noted in the expense system.
For accommodation: Hotel folio (itemised bill) — room rate and tax only; mini-bar, personal calls, spa services are not reimbursable.
For mileage: Date, origin, destination, distance, and business purpose.

4.3 Submission Process

Submit expenses through the Meridian Expense system at expenses.meridiantech.com. Upload original receipts (photo or PDF). Claims are processed within 7 working days of manager approval. Reimbursement is made via bank transfer on the 15th of the following month.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

5. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

The following are NEVER reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment (movies, personal travel, sightseeing)
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or penalties of any kind
- Gifts to colleagues (covered separately under HR gift policy)
- Personal grooming, clothing (unless mandatory uniform)
- Alcohol at internal events
- Expenses for family members or guests unless explicitly approved
- Losses due to theft or negligence

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6. CORPORATE CREDIT CARD

Senior employees (Manager level and above) may be issued a corporate credit card for travel and entertainment expenses. The card must only be used for business expenses. Personal charges on the corporate card must be reimbursed within 5 business days. Repeated personal use of the corporate card will result in card revocation and disciplinary action.

Monthly card statements must be reconciled and submitted with receipts by the 5th of the following month.
